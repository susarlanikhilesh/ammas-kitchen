# Rasam (చారు)

## Ingredients
- Lemon-size piece of tamarind (చింతపండు)
- Salt (ఉప్పు)
- Tomato (టమాటా), cut
- Drumstick (మునగకాయ), if you have it
- 2 or 3 green chillies (పచ్చి మిరపకాయలు)
- Turmeric (పసుపు)
- Curry leaves (కరివేపాకు)
- 1½ spoons of rasam powder (చారు పొడి)
- Coriander (కొత్తిమీర)
- 2 or 3 glasses of water (నీళ్ళు)
- For the tempering (పోపు): very little oil (నూనె), mustard seeds (ఆవాలు), a little cumin (జీలకర్ర), hing (ఇంగువ) [unconfirmed], 2 red chillies (ఎండు మిరపకాయలు)

## Instructions
1. Put everything except the tempering into a vessel.
2. Add the water and let it boil nicely.
3. After switching off, heat the oil, add the tempering items and pour them over the rasam.
