# Cashew-Onion-Tomato Masala Base

## Ingredients
- A little oil (నూనె)
- Small piece of cinnamon (దాల్చిన చెక్క)
- 2 cloves (లవంగాలు)
- 1 cardamom (యాలకులు)
- Ginger-garlic paste (అల్లం వెల్లుల్లి ముద్ద), or 5 garlic cloves and a small piece of ginger, cut
- 2 medium onions (ఉల్లిపాయలు), cut long
- 7 or 8 cashews (జీడిపప్పు)
- Cumin powder (జీలకర్ర పొడి)
- Coriander powder (ధనియాల పొడి)
- Garam masala
- Turmeric (పసుపు)
- Chilli powder (కారం)
- Salt (ఉప్పు)
- 2 or 3 medium tomatoes (టమాటాలు), cut in cubes

## Instructions
1. Heat the oil and add the cinnamon, cloves and cardamom.
2. Add the onions and fry nicely, then add the ginger-garlic paste and fry nicely.
3. Add the cashews, then the cumin powder, coriander powder, garam masala, turmeric, chilli powder and salt. Fry nicely.
4. Add the tomatoes and cook till they're soft.
5. Switch off, let it cool and grind.
