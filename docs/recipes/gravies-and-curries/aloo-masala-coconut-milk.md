# Aloo Masala (ఆలూ మసాలా)

## Ingredients
- Potatoes (బంగాళదుంపలు), peeled and cut in big pieces
- Tomatoes (టమాటాలు), cut in big pieces
- 2 onions (ఉల్లిపాయలు), long and thin
- Coconut milk (కొబ్బరి పాలు)
- Coconut paste (కొబ్బరి ముద్ద)
- Oil (నూనె)
- 2 cloves (లవంగాలు)
- 1 cardamom (యాలకులు)
- Small piece of cinnamon (దాల్చిన చెక్క)
- Cumin (జీలకర్ర)
- Ginger-garlic paste (అల్లం వెల్లుల్లి ముద్ద)
- Turmeric (పసుపు)
- Green chillies (పచ్చి మిరపకాయలు)
- Chilli powder (కారం)
- Garam masala powder
- Salt (ఉప్పు)
- A little water (నీళ్ళు)
- Finely chopped coriander (కొత్తిమీర)

## Instructions
1. In the pressure pan, heat the oil and add the cloves, cardamom, cinnamon and cumin. Add the onions and fry till a little brown.
2. Add the ginger-garlic paste and fry. Add the coconut paste and fry until it becomes a lump (ముద్ద).
3. Add the turmeric, green chillies, chilli powder and garam masala, then the potatoes and tomatoes. Mix nicely.
4. Add the coconut milk, a little water and salt. Taste it and mix.
5. Cook for 2 whistles. Once the lid is off, add the coriander.
