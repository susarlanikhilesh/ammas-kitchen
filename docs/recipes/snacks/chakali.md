# Chakali (చకలి)

## Ingredients
- 1 cup rice flour
- 1 cup water
-  salt
-  jeera
-  hing
-  chilli powder
- 2 spoons oil

## Instructions
1. Heat water, salt, jeera, hing, chilli powder, 2 spoons oil
2. Once water is hot boiled, off it
3. Slowly put rice flour
4. Slowly mix it & mazgukovali
5. Once warm, take it & make into shape
6. Boil water, put all the items
7. Put 2 spoons oil
8. Then put rice flour
9. Once warm, make flat shape
10. Take 2 plastic sheets put middle & make shape
