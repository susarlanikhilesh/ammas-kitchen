# Chegodilu (చేగోడీలు)

## Ingredients
- 1 cup rice flour (బియ్యప్పిండి)
- 1 cup water (నీళ్ళు)
- Salt (ఉప్పు)
- Cumin (జీలకర్ర)
- Moong dal (పెసరపప్పు)
- Hing (ఇంగువ)
- Chilli powder (కారం)
- 2 spoons oil (నూనె)

## Instructions
1. Heat the water with the salt, cumin, moong dal, hing, chilli powder and oil.
2. Once the water has boiled, switch it off.
3. Slowly put in the rice flour and slowly mix it.
4. Once it's warm, take it and shape it.
