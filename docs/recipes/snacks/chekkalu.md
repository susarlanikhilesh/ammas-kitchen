# Chekkalu (చెక్కలు)

## Ingredients
- 1 cup rice flour (బియ్యప్పిండి)
- 1 cup water (నీళ్ళు)
- Ginger-chilli paste (అల్లం మిర్చి ముద్ద)
- Curry leaves (కరివేపాకు)
- Cumin (జీలకర్ర)
- 2 spoons oil (నూనె)
- Moong dal (పెసరపప్పు) and chana dal (శనగపప్పు), soaked for ½ hour

## Instructions
1. Boil the water and put in all the items, including 2 spoons of oil.
2. Then put in the rice flour.
3. Once it's warm, make flat shapes: put a ball between 2 plastic sheets and press it out.
