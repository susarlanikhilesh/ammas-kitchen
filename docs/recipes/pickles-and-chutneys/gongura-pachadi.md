# Gongura Pachadi (గోంగూర పచ్చడి)

## Ingredients
- 2 spoons of fenugreek seeds (మెంతులు)
- Dry red chillies (ఎండు మిరపకాయలు), as many as you want for spice

    !!! question "Not sure — check with Amma"
        The number at 0:08 is cut off

- About 4 spoons of oil (నూనె) for the gongura, plus a little more for the tempering
- Gongura leaves (గోంగూర)
- Salt (ఉప్పు), the usual amount
- For the tempering (పోపు): mustard seeds (ఆవాలు), hing (ఇంగువ), peeled garlic (వెల్లుల్లిపాయలు)
- Chilli powder (కారం పొడి), if you want it spicier

## Instructions
1. First put the kadai (మూకుడు) on. Add 2 spoons of fenugreek, then the red chillies, as many as you want for spice. Fry till dark brown and keep aside.
2. In the same kadai, add about 4 spoons of oil and the gongura. Put the lid on a little and fry. At the same time add salt, the usual amount.

    !!! question "Not sure — check with Amma"
        At 0:30: something you can mix in "if you want"

3. Keep it on sim and fry till the gongura becomes soft and thick (దగ్గర పడుతుంది). Take it out and keep aside.
4. In the same kadai, add a little oil again, then mustard seeds, hing and the peeled garlic. Fry the garlic and keep aside. This is the popu.
5. First, in the mixer, grind the fried fenugreek and red chillies coarsely. Take it out and keep aside.

    !!! question "Not sure — check with Amma"
        At 0:58: coarse or powder

6. In the same mixer, add the gongura and half of the fried garlic, and grind it smooth.
7. Add the fenugreek-chilli powder you made, and chilli powder too, and mix.
8. Mix it all into the same popu. That's it, gongura pachadi is ready.
9. After that, if you want more spice, add more chilli powder. If the salt is less, add a little and mix.
