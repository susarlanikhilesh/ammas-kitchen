# Gongura Pickle (గోంగూర పచ్చడి)

## Ingredients
- 2 spoons Fenugreek seeds (Methulu) (మెంతులు)
- Dry red chillies (ఎండు మిరపకాయలు), as needed
- 2 drops + 4-5 spoons + little Oil (నూనె)
- Gongura leaves (గోంగూర), Voice
- Salt (ఉప్పు), to taste
- Mustard seeds (Aavalu) (ఆవాలు), Bunch
- Asafoetida (Inguva) (ఇంగువ), Bunch
- Garlic cloves (వెల్లుల్లి పాయలు), Bunch

## Instructions
1. In a pan first add 2 spoons of fenugreek seeds.
2. Add dry red chillies (as much as you want for spice) and 2 drops of oil.
3. Fry until fenugreek turns dark brown. Keep aside.
4. In the same pan add 4-5 spoons of oil and gongura leaves.
5. Cover with lid and cook. Gongura will shrink.
6. Add salt to taste. You can mix from top if needed.
7. Cook on low flame until gongura becomes soft and comes together. Keep this aside.
8. In the same pan add a little oil, mustard seeds, asafoetida and crushed garlic cloves.
9. Fry until garlic turns red. Keep aside.
10. In mixer jar first add the fried fenugreek seeds and dry red chillies. Make powder and keep aside.
11. In the same mixer jar add the cooked gongura and half of the fried garlic. Grind to a coarse paste.
12. Add the prepared chilli-fenugreek powder as much as needed and mix.
13. Finally mix everything with the prepared popu (tadka).
