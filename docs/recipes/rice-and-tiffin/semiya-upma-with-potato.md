# Semiya with Potato (సేమియా)

## Ingredients
- Vermicelli (సేమియా), plus 2 drops of oil
- Ginger-garlic paste (అల్లం వెల్లుల్లి ముద్ద)
- Green chillies (పచ్చి మిరపకాయలు)
- 2 to 3 spoons of oil (నూనె)
- 2 cloves (లవంగాలు)
- 1 cardamom (యాలకులు)
- Small piece of cinnamon (దాల్చిన చెక్క)
- Onion (ఉల్లిపాయ), cut long
- Curry leaves (కరివేపాకు)
- Potato (బంగాళదుంప)
- Water (నీళ్ళు)
- Salt (ఉప్పు)

## Instructions
1. Fry the vermicelli in 2 drops of oil and keep it aside.
2. Grind the ginger-garlic paste and green chillies together into a paste.
3. Heat the oil and add the cloves, cardamom, cinnamon and onion. Fry.
4. Add the chilli paste and curry leaves and fry, then add the potato and fry.
5. Add water and salt and taste it. Let it boil until the potato breaks.
6. Add the vermicelli. Don't put the lid on, and mix nicely.
