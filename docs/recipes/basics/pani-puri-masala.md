# Pani Puri Masala (పానీపూరి మసాలా)

## Ingredients
- 2 spoons coriander seeds (ధనియా)
- 1 spoon cumin seeds (జీరా)
- black pepper (మిరియాలు), half spoon
- 2-3 cloves (లవంగాలు)
- 1-2 dry red chillies (ఎండు మిరపకాయలు)

## Instructions
1. Take 2 spoons coriander seeds, 1 spoon jeera, half spoon miriyalu, 2-3 lavangalu and 1-2 endu mirapakayalu.
2. Roast them well.
3. Make it into powder.
