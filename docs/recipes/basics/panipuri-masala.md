# Panipuri Masala (పానీపూరి మసాలా)

## Ingredients
- 2 spoons of coriander seeds (ధనియాలు)
- 1 spoon of cumin (జీలకర్ర)
- ½ spoon of black pepper (మిరియాలు)
- 2 or 3 cloves (లవంగాలు)
- 1 or 2 dry red chillies (ఎండు మిరపకాయలు)
- A piece of ginger (అల్లం)

## Instructions
1. Take 2 spoons of coriander seeds, 1 spoon of cumin and ½ spoon of pepper. Then add 2 or 3 cloves and 1 or 2 red chillies.
2. Add a piece of ginger too.

    !!! question "Not sure — check with Amma"
        In a separate 3-second "sorry" note, she doesn't say when to add it

3. Fry well and make it into a powder.
